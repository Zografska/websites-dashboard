# Privacy and tracking

This page explains which data is processed when you read this report, and which choices you have. It is given under Art. 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

## Who processes the data

- **Data controller:** [Data controller, e.g. University / Department, address]
- **Data protection officer (DPO):** [DPO contact]
- **Contact for the study:** [name and institutional email]

## Why we process data

The report is part of a research study by [institution] on environmental claims on company websites. With your consent we measure **how much the report is used**, to understand whether and how firms use it. The measurement is optional: the report works the same whether you accept or decline.

## What we record, if you accept

- that the report was opened, and which sections were viewed (Overview, Claim examples, How to read it, Methodology);
- which claims are opened, identified by a technical code and not by their text;
- clicks to the source pages on the firm's website and to the website itself (the click, not the address);
- use of the filters and of the language switch;
- the time the page is actively used;
- the interface language and whether the screen is narrow or wide;
- when you accepted, and the version of this notice.

This data is linked to the **firm** through a pseudonymous code, not to the person reading the report. Several people at the same firm are counted together.

## What we do not record

IP addresses, names, email addresses, the firm's name or identifier, page addresses, claim text, screen or session recordings, data from other sites. We use no cookies and no other persistent identifiers: the visit identifier stays in memory and is lost when the page is closed or reloaded.

## Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 122 of the Italian Data Protection Code). You can withdraw it at any time at the bottom of this page: the measurement stops immediately. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

## Who receives the data

- **PostHog Inc.**, as data processor, stores the measurement data on its cloud platform in the European Union (Frankfurt). [Reference to the data processing agreement and transfer safeguards]
- **GitHub Inc.** hosts the site (GitHub Pages) and, as for every hosted site, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub stores nothing on your device, and the research team has no access to these logs.
- Only the researchers in charge can access the measurement data: [who has access].

## Your rights

You can ask the controller for access to the data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and you can withdraw your consent, by writing to the contacts above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the [Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali)](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).
